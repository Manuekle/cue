Will people see Cue when I share my screen?
No. On Mac the prompter window is excluded from screen capture, so it doesn't appear in Zoom, Meet, FaceTime or screen recordings. Only you can see it.
How does it know I started recording on iPhone?
iOS doesn't tell apps when another app records. While the floating window is open, Cue keeps a light audio session. When another app starts recording, iOS interrupts that session, and Cue takes it as the signal to count down and start. You can always tap ▶ yourself.
Which apps start it automatically on Mac?
Any app that turns on the camera: Zoom, Google Meet, FaceTime, QuickTime, OBS, Photo Booth and more. You can also make the microphone the trigger.
Is the Copilot private?
Transcription runs on your Mac with Whisper. With Apple Intelligence or Ollama (for example qwen2.5:7b) the replies are generated locally too. Cloud models like OpenAI, Gemini or Claude are optional and use your own key, stored in the Keychain.
What do I need to run it?
A Mac with macOS 14 or later, or an iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later. The notch tab works best on a Mac with a notch; on other Macs it sits just under the menu bar. Apple Intelligence needs macOS 26.
